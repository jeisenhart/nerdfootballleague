Recruiting Guidelines

Official protocols, pipelines, and constraints for building your dynasty roster.

Regular Season Recruiting

Guidelines during the active weekly season cycles:

  • Once season begins not allowed to add 4 and 5 star players.
  • Once season begins can add 3 star players up to and including week 8.
  • After week 8 only 2 star and below are allowed to be added.

Transfer Portal Guidelines

Guidelines during the transfer portal acquisitions:

  • If you still have offers out to 5 star recruits and they have not committed to anyone at the time the portal start they will be treated as commitments and count towards your allotment. Example: If you have 2 5 stars committed and 2 more are still undecided out of you original 7 then you cannot add another 5 star in portal
