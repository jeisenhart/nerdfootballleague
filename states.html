Pipeline & Territory Management

When targeting prospects outside of your program's immediate home state, coaches must consider regional proximity bonuses and pipeline availability:

  • Primary pipelines automatically grant baseline scouting and weekly interest multipliers.
  • Neighboring states share secondary pipeline bleed effects depending on your program tier and coach specialization levels.
  • Coordinate with conference rivals to avoid redundant resource allocation in overlapping border zones.
